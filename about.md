Welcome to your world. This page is the world overview — your world's *framing*: the elevator pitch, the tone you're going for, and the hard rules of the setting (no gunpowder? magic is feared?). Keep it brief and world-level — the details of specific people, places, and groups belong in entities, not here.

Entities live in folders by type — continents, regions, settlements, buildings, sites, guilds, and NPCs — and each folder has its own about page for that section's framing. The `.meta` folder defines what fields each entity type has; edit those templates freely as your world grows.

To turn a pile of unstructured notes into entities, use **Import notes** rather than pasting them here. When you chat with the assistant, "Include world overview" grounds it in this page — so keeping it lean keeps that grounding sharp.
